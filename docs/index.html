Trust Layer

Adversarial multi-agent verification for B2B payment fraud. We intercept every incoming invoice and run it through our agentic OS. At the end, you get a score, and a human decides.

Six independent sources.
One confident decision.

Zwapgrid LIVE DATA

Invoice and payment history, pulled from Zwapgrid's integrated ERP systems. Reports back to Zwapgrid once the invoice has been paid through Open Payments.

Bankgirot VERIFIED SOURCE

Account and payment validation: checks whether an account number is valid, then looks up who owns it in the public register.

Account Registry SIMULATED

Account ownership lookup, to understand the company behind the account.

Open Payments PAYMENT NETWORK

Live payment execution: initiates and pays the invoice pulled in through Zwapgrid and its integrated ERP systems, a real payment, not a simulation.

Bolagsverket STUB READY

Business identity checks: company status, address and registration from official records. Free public API, we're still waiting on access.

Relationship Intelligence ENRICHED INSIGHTS

Background signals and past dealings: public sources combined with our own records of every past invoice from this supplier.

Six independent sources means no single wrong answer sinks the case: the pipeline reaches a decision faster, with more confidence, and a clear trail of why.
